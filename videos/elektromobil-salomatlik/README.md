# Elektr avtomobillar erkaklar salomatligiga zararlimi? (3d-skill, style #4)

66 s, 10 chapters, Adizzbek voice, handle @autopodveska.uz. This video was built with `skills/3d-skill`.

**Facts and sources** (checked 2026-11-24):
- SINTEF / EM-Safety (Norway): in electric cars, the magnetic field at the feet is at most about 20 % of the ICNIRP
  reference level (at start-up), and at head height about 2 %. Petrol cars reach about 10 % at the feet.
- Jung et al., *Fertility and Sterility* 2008: one hour on a heated car seat raised the average scrotal temperature
  to 37.3 °C (36.7 °C unheated). The study did **not** measure sperm, hence the script's "ta'sir qilishi mumkin".
- Testes sit about 2-3 °C below core temperature.
